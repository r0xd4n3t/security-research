# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep this research accurate and useful to defenders.

## Ways to contribute

- **Corrections** — flag an inaccurate or overstated claim with a supporting
  reference. Accuracy fixes are the most valued contribution here.
- **Detection and mitigation** — suggest additional, vendor-neutral detection
  or hardening guidance grounded in the documented behavior.
- **Reproducibility feedback** — report where a documented validation was hard
  to follow or reproduce.

## Quality bar

- Claims must be supported by retained evidence or an authoritative reference.
- Contributions must stay within the defensive scope in
  [`METHODOLOGY.md`](METHODOLOGY.md): no weaponized exploit code and no
  public-target testing.
- Keep language precise about what was independently validated versus what is
  reported upstream.

## How to propose a change

Open an issue or a pull request describing the change and the evidence behind
it. Please keep each change focused on a single advisory or topic.
